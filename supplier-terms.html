Supplier Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms carefully before creating your YADHUVII supplier account.

Important Notice

These Supplier Terms & Conditions describe the general responsibilities and expectations between YADHUVII and participating suppliers. By continuing, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these terms.

01

Supplier Eligibility

Suppliers must provide accurate and complete business information when applying to become a YADHUVII supplier.

  • Supplier information must be truthful and current.
  • Suppliers must be legally permitted to sell their products.
  • YADHUVII may review supplier applications before approval.
02

Supplier Approval

Creating a supplier account does not automatically guarantee approval.

YADHUVII may review the supplier's business information, products, quality standards and ability to fulfil orders before approving the account.

03

Product Authenticity & Quality

Suppliers are responsible for ensuring that all products submitted to YADHUVII are authentic, lawful and accurately represented.

  • Products must match their descriptions.
  • Products must meet reasonable quality standards.
  • Counterfeit or unlawfully supplied products are prohibited.
  • Suppliers must inform YADHUVII of material product issues.
04

Product Information

Suppliers must provide accurate information for every product submitted to YADHUVII.

  • Product name
  • Description
  • Images
  • Price
  • Available stock
  • Variants and specifications

Suppliers must promptly update information when products, prices or availability change.

05

Orders & Fulfilment

Customer orders placed through YADHUVII may be forwarded to the relevant supplier for fulfilment according to the applicable supplier process.

Suppliers are responsible for preparing and fulfilling accepted orders within the agreed timeframe.

  • Orders should be processed promptly.
  • Products should be correctly prepared and packaged.
  • Suppliers should provide accurate fulfilment information.
  • Unexpected delays must be communicated to YADHUVII.
06

Stock Availability

Suppliers are responsible for keeping stock information reasonably accurate.

If a product becomes unavailable after an order has been received, the supplier must notify YADHUVII as soon as reasonably possible.

07

Packaging & Delivery

Suppliers must package products appropriately for delivery and follow the fulfilment and shipping arrangements agreed with YADHUVII.

Responsibility for shipping, packaging and handover may vary depending on the supplier agreement and order arrangement.

08

Returns, Defective Products & Customer Issues

Suppliers must cooperate with YADHUVII when an order involves a defective, damaged, incorrect or materially misrepresented product.

Return, replacement, refund and related responsibilities will be handled according to the applicable YADHUVII policy and supplier agreement.

09

Supplier Payments

Supplier payments and settlement arrangements will be determined according to the commercial terms agreed between YADHUVII and the supplier.

Suppliers are responsible for providing accurate payment information where required.

10

Customer Information & Privacy

Any customer information provided to a supplier for order fulfilment must be used only for legitimate fulfilment and service purposes.

Suppliers must not misuse, sell, share or independently market to customers using information obtained through YADHUVII.

11

Intellectual Property

Suppliers must have the necessary rights or permissions to provide product images, descriptions, logos and other materials submitted to YADHUVII.

Suppliers must not submit materials that unlawfully infringe the rights of another person or business.

12

Prohibited Products

Suppliers may not submit products that violate applicable laws, regulations or YADHUVII policies.

YADHUVII may reject or remove products that it considers inappropriate, unsafe, unlawful or unsuitable for the marketplace.

13

Account Review & Suspension

YADHUVII may review supplier accounts and product listings from time to time.

Accounts or products may be suspended, restricted or removed where there are serious quality, fulfilment, compliance, customer-service or policy concerns.

14

Ending the Partnership

Either party may request to end the supplier relationship subject to any applicable commercial agreement or outstanding order obligations.

Ending the partnership does not automatically remove responsibilities relating to orders or matters that arose before termination.

15

Changes to These Terms

YADHUVII may update these supplier terms from time to time as the platform, business operations or applicable requirements develop.

Where appropriate, suppliers may be required to review and accept updated terms before continuing to use supplier services.

16

Agreement

By accepting these terms, the supplier confirms that the information provided during registration is accurate and that the supplier agrees to comply with the applicable YADHUVII supplier requirements.
